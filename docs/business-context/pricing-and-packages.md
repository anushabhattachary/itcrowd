# Pricing and Packages

## Current model: three monthly retainer tiers

This is the pricing in the July 2026 investor deck and the partnership templates Chris sends to clients. The website's "$500-$2,000 typical monthly spend" claim comes from here and is accurate.

### Local: $500/month
"Perfect for a first campaign."
- 1 creator (micro-influencer or athlete)
- 2 posts/month (feed + story)
- Creator vetting and briefing
- Monthly performance report, strategy notes
- Company dashboard, campaign activation and management

### Growth: $1,000/month (positioned as most popular / best value)
- 3 micro-creators, rotated monthly
- 6 posts/month across creators
- Seasonal content planning
- Dedicated account manager
- Everything in Local

### Anchor: $2,000/month
"Full local dominance."
- 5-8 creators (micro + mid tier)
- 12+ posts/month
- Seasonal campaign creative direction
- Priority creator access and scheduling
- Quarterly strategy review

### Add-ons that have been floated internally (not firm commitments; don't put on the site without checking)
- Event boost: $350 one-time (creator posts around a specific date)
- Reel package: $350/month for 2 extra dedicated reels
- Creator collab day: $500 one-time on-site shoot with 2-3 creators
- Referral: a small business referring another gets a month free
- Event booth: $150-400/hr

## Unit economics context (internal only, never publish)

Creator payouts are deliberately low and capped: around $40-60 cash plus a meal for food-and-drink campaigns, up to a few hundred dollars per video for larger creators (internal cap discussed: $300/video, negotiable down). Known real rates from the pipeline: local foodie accounts around $450-800/month; a 363k-follower foodie quoted $5-8k (too expensive; the kind of creator ItCrowd avoids). Deals are cash; some creators work on affiliate links or product.
