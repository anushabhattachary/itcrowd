# Business Overview

## What ItCrowd is

ItCrowd LLC is an influencer-marketing service founded at Georgia Tech. It matches small businesses and startups (mostly Atlanta-area) with vetted local creators: micro-influencers, NIL and amateur athletes, foodies, photographers, and videographers. ItCrowd handles the whole campaign: sourcing, vetting, outreach, negotiation, contracts, scheduling, monitoring, and reporting. The pitch line the founders actually use: "Real businesses. Real creators. Real results."

## The model, and how it evolved

1. **Original concept ("InCrowd", 2025):** a VC-flavored idea where influencers promoted startups in exchange for equity ("sweat equity"). Internal docs from this era talk heavily about equity, SAFE notes, and vesting.
2. **Hybrid era (early-mid 2026):** cash, equity, or blended deals. The 5/11/26 pricing doc still says "all deals can be made as equity hybrids."
3. **Current (as of July 2026): cash only.** Businesses pay a monthly retainer; creators are paid cash per deliverable. Equity was dropped as a payment method and must not appear anywhere user-facing.

Revenue comes from monthly retainers (see pricing-and-packages.md). An earlier internal model targeted a 15% margin on influencer payments; the retainer model supersedes it. Creator cost is kept low and capped (for food-and-drink campaigns, roughly $40-60 cash per creator plus a meal), which is what makes the retainers high-margin.

## Target customer (ICP)

- B2C businesses with a website and something concrete to sell: restaurants, local shops, consumer apps, DTC products.
- Startups from the Georgia Tech ecosystem (Create-X, ATDC, Nexus) and other accelerators.
- Local "settled" small businesses that can afford $500-$2,000/month (a deliberate pivot from broke early-stage startups toward businesses that can pay).

On the creator side, ItCrowd deliberately avoids agency-represented and expensive creators, and prioritizes niche fit over follower count.

## Positioning

Against agencies: agencies cost $10K-$50K+ per month and move slowly; ItCrowd is $500-$2,000 and live in under two weeks. Against marketplaces and DIY: those leave the business to figure everything out; ItCrowd is fully managed. The wedge is affordable, local-matched, fully managed campaigns for businesses no one else builds for, plus NIL athletes as a differentiated creator pool.

## Software direction (from the founders' "Future look" doc)

The website plus dashboards should evolve toward: intake forms feeding a matchmaking database, businesses seeing proposed creator matches (like a shortlist preview) before booking, and live campaign metrics for businesses. Think "we drive partnerships with our network of creators," business-side first (a cited competitor, sideshift.app, focuses on the creator side instead).
