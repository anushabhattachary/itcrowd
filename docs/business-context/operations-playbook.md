# How Campaigns Actually Run

This is the operational reality behind the marketing site and the dashboards. Keep site copy and dashboard features consistent with it.

## Campaign cycle (repeatable 5-week cycle, fully managed)

- **Week 1: Discovery and match.** Goals call (product, audience, goals, budget). Handpicked creator shortlist begins; the handbook standard is 5-10 vetted creators with follower counts, rates, and profiles.
- **Week 2: Approve and schedule.** Business picks creators. ItCrowd runs outreach, contracts, and the calendar.
- **Weeks 3-4: Create and post.** Reels, stories, tags go live on schedule; ItCrowd monitors.
- **Week 5: Report and renew.** Full performance report (exposure, metrics, what worked and what didn't) and the renewal conversation.

Sales claim used publicly: intro call to live campaign in under two weeks. Shortlists are typically delivered within 5-7 days.

## Standard deliverables per creator

- 1 TikTok or Instagram Reel
- 1-3 story posts
- Business tags and mentions, link inclusion when applicable
- Content must include the business name, quality visuals, atmosphere footage, honest creator experience, and a clear call to action
- Typical cadence: 2-4 posts per creator per month

Optional extras: multiple videos, professional photography, giveaways, long-term ambassador partnerships.

## Creator sourcing rules (from the acquisition handbook)

- Avoid creators represented by big agencies (inflated prices); negotiate rates.
- Target the client's niche; fit beats follower count.
- Micro and macro both fine; engagement and niche fit matter most.
- Keep unpicked creators warm ("we'll keep them in the system").
- Key principles: volume drives opportunity, don't force misaligned deals, speed matters but quality control matters more, relationships compound.

## Client acquisition motion

LinkedIn-first founder outreach (Chris), customer-discovery style: lead with wanting to learn about the business, let exposure problems surface, then offer a shortlist within a month. Startup sources: Product Hunt (D2C), F6S, ATDC graduating classes, Create-X. Intake runs through Google Forms today ("ItCrowd x Company interest form" and "Influencer/Creator Info"), whose responses land in Sheets; the long-term plan is to move this intake into the website with the same questions feeding the matching database.

## Advice the founders are acting on (Jen Whitlow meeting)

Metrics-based, standardized contracts; automated vetting and performance tracking; deposit upfront with remainder tied to metrics; retention over one-off deals. When building dashboard features, performance tracking and reporting are the highest-value directions.
